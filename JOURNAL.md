# 11/04/2026 - routing the keybit flex

ok so today i finished routing the whole new keybit. its a cross shaped flex with the CH592F in the middle and a flap on each side that folds up into the case, and each flap has 6 magnet pads going N S N S N S plus its own little switch circuit. i routed the north flap first, then copied it to the other 3 sides and relinked the copies to their own parts in the schematic (south, east and west) so every side has its own nets.

![image](assets/journal/v2/keybit_v2_full_routing.png)

the tricky part is the magnet voltage. since you can put a key next to another one in any rotation or stagger you dont know which magnet net (A or B) is gonna be + and which is gnd. so each side has 2 p-fets (RV2C014BCT2CL) that connect a net to VBUS and 2 n-fets (SSM3K56ACT) that connect a net to gnd, with the gates crossed. whichever net is higher turns on the p-fet to VBUS on its side and the n-fet to gnd on the other side, so it acts like a bridge rectifier but without losing 0.6V per key like diodes would. then 2 more small n-fets on an EN pin let the CH592 decide if a side is allowed to send power out, while a dead key can still get power in through the body diodes. the 1M resistors keep the magnets at 0V when nothing touches them, and 2 RB520 diodes into a 100k/300k divider let each side sense if its getting power, which is how the keyword can figure out who is next to who.

![image](assets/journal/v2/keybit_v2_side_schematic.png)

all the transistors are DFN1006 (1x0.6mm) which is TINY but its the only way all of this fits on a ~5mm flap. heres a closer look at the south flap and the middle with the CH592, the crystal and the antenna in the corner with a keepout around it

![image](assets/journal/v2/keybit_v2_flap_routing.png)

i also had to move a bunch of traces away from the folds since anything running along a bend will crack, and i switched the board to jlc's flex rules (1/3 oz copper, 0.025mm polyimide) so drc checks against what they can actually make.

keybit-hall - PCBWay order notes

Board: 2-layer flex, polyimide core 25um, copper 1/3 oz (12um) both sides, coverlay both sides, ENIG, about 0.1mm total without stiffener.
Stiffener: FR4 0.2mm on the TOP side (the side with the switch). Outline and cutouts are in "keybit-hall-User_Drawings.gbr" (KiCad User.Drawings layer). Outer closed outlines are stiffener pieces (1 middle + 4 flaps), inner closed outlines are cutouts with no stiffener.
Stiffener edges stop about 0.7mm or more before each fold line so the flaps can bend 90 degrees. Please keep them as drawn.
The same note is on "keybit-hall-User_Comments.gbr".
Assembly: parts on both sides (most on the bottom).

ANT1 (Kinghelm KH-1608-H08 chip antenna, bottom side): the chip has a black band on one end. The band end goes on pad 1 (the unconnected pad, marked by the filled silk block on the board). Pad 2 is the RF feed and must get the end WITHOUT the band.
